# Lesson 7: Public Sponsor 💖

**Badge:** sponsor a person or organization through **GitHub Sponsors**. One-time.

## The real skill: understanding how open source is funded

Almost every project you use is maintained by people, often volunteers. GitHub Sponsors lets you pay them directly, monthly or one-time.

## Exercise

1. Look at a project you depend on. Find the **♡ Sponsor** button on its repo page, or check its `.github/FUNDING.yml`.
2. Or browse [github.com/sponsors/explore](https://github.com/sponsors/explore). It can suggest maintainers of your dependencies.
3. Choose a tier. Many accept **$1/month** or a **one-time** amount.
4. Keep the sponsorship **public** if you want it to show on your profile. Private sponsorships may not display the badge.

> 💡 Can't spend money right now? That's completely fine. Skip this badge. Starring, writing docs,
> filing good bug reports and answering questions are all real support too.

## Bonus: get sponsored yourself

If you maintain something useful, set up your own profile at
[github.com/sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors) and add a `.github/FUNDING.yml`:

```yaml
github: [your-username]
```
